New ways of seeing

How do we, as educators, deal with the AI revolution?

Human history contains only a handful of technological revolutions powerful enough to reorganise society itself. Fire reshaped hunting societies. Agriculture transformed human settlement. The wheel expanded trade. Writing systems and the printing press gave rise to literate societies. Combustion engines accelerated industrialisation. Computing created digital societies. The internet shaped knowledge societies. Now, we have artificial intelligence (AI).

In this module, you will explore the history, purpose and growing influence of AI in education. You will learn key definitions, examine major developments in the field, and consider how AI is reshaping teaching and learning. You will also be introduced to the Australian Framework for Generative AI in Schools, which emphasises responsible use, transparency, fairness, accountability and student wellbeing, as well as other global educational frameworks.

Your expert, Dr Luke Rowe, introduces this module in the following video. Don’t forget to take notes!

The learning outcomes for this module

  • Outline major milestones shaping AI in education.
  • Recognise opportunities and risks of using AI in teaching and learning.
  • Identify core principles from the Australian Framework for Generative AI in Schools that guide responsible use.
