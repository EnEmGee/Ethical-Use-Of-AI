About this course

Artificial intelligence (AI) is not a mere extension of existing tools but a shift that accelerates how information is produced, evaluated and interacted with, demanding new cognitive and educational responses. It can enhance learning design, support diverse learners, and reduce workload associated with repetitive or highly structured tasks. Yet AI also raises important classroom questions about accuracy, bias, privacy, wellbeing, appropriate dependence and academic integrity.
